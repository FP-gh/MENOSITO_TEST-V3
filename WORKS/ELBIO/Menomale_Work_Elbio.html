Elbio

  • Breathable backpack panels
  • 2024
  • NABA, Nuova Accademia di Belle Arti, Milano

Elbio redefines comfort on the trail: positioned between the backpack and your back, it improves breathability and reduces sweating so you can enjoy every step with freedom.
The relationship between your body and the backpack changes: an upper and a lower panel ensure proper load distribution.
The Springtiles, freely positionable blocks that adjust to follow the body's movements, create space between the backpack and your back, reducing sweating.
Versatile and adaptable, from trekking to urban life.
The springtiles are designed using a three-dimensional Voronoi pattern optimized through iterative testing and are printed in TPU using 3D Polyjet technology.
The elastic, perforated structure allows for controlled deformation, facilitating ventilation 3D Voronoi of the area covered by the backpack.
