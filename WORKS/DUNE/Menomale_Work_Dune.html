Dune

  • Umbrella stand
  • 2025
  • Design Wanted, Open Call #3

How many times have you set down your umbrella on a rainy day and wondered whether you’d still find it there the next time? Some everyday objects, by their very nature, need to be stored within everyone’s reach; but that doesn’t mean they shouldn’t be kept safe. Dune is an umbrella stand designed for shared spaces where groups of people coexist without necessarily knowing each other well, like large residential buildings or offices. With its combination lock mechanism, Dune keeps umbrellas safe from strangers while maintaining an overall functionality and an elegant design that adds character and style to the surrounding space. Dune’s handle has a combination lock that grants the umbrella’s safety between the users who can share the password within their group. To change the combination, users simply need to press the reset button after entering the old numbers and then choose a new one.
