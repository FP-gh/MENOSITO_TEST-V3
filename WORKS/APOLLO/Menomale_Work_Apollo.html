Apollo

  • Paperweight
  • 2025
  • Menomale Design Studio

Apollo is our first project—not just a symbol, but a tangible creation. It’s our way of saying: let’s really start here. Designed between calls, a few sketches, and a couple of too many doubts, it was created using laser cutting on brushed steel sheet metal. Slim and just the right weight, it holds papers, notes, and receipts securely in place.
