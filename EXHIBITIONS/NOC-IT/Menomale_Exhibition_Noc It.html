Noc It

  • Nutcracker
  • September 2025
  • About: Schiaccianoci by Sovrappensiero

The theme of the About Archivio open call, organized by Sovrappensiero Design, is: “Nutcracker.”
Cracking a nut is more than an everyday act, it is a display of strength, and the stage is the dining table.
The spectators wait, trembling with excitement.
The tension in the muscles. The silence of the blow. The sound that bursts forth. The applause.
Noc It combines the functionality of a nutcracker with the excitement of the Luna Park game, Strongman.
The result is a sharp blow, a nut to eat, and the ringing of the bell.
