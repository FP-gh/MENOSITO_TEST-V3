Hootch

  • Tape cutter ring
  • December 2025
  • About: Tagliascotch by Sovrappensiero

In a workshop where ribbons run wild and bows act up, you need a quick helper.
That's how Hootch was born: two rings on your fingers, your thumb guiding the way, and the ribbon gliding obediently along until it's time for the snip!
The sharp micro-blades cut cleanly, the motion stays smooth, and your hands stay free.
When worn, Hootch isn't a tool: it's a little workmate.
It works with you, speeds up every step, and adds a touch of artisanal magic to every well-made package.
